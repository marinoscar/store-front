07

FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Do you offer free quotes?+

Yes — every quote is free and there's no obligation to book. Use the form on this page or call us; we'll typically reply within one business day.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Fully licensed contractor, liability insurance up to $2M, plus workers' comp. Docs on request.

How long does a project take?+

Depends on scope. Single-room repaint 1–2 days, deck rebuild 3–5 days, flooring varies. The quote includes a written timeline.

Do I need to be home?+

Not necessarily. Many customers give us a key or smart-lock code. We send daily progress photos either way.

What's your payment schedule?+

Small deposit on signing, midpoint payment, final on completion and walk-through. Bank transfer and credit cards accepted.

What if I'm not happy?+

Not done until you've walked through and signed off. If there's an issue after, we come back and fix it — workmanship warranty.

Do you handle permits?+

When required (structural, major electrical), we handle the application and inspection scheduling and roll the cost into the quote.
