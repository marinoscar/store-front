Frequently asked

The questions we hear most often.

Do you offer free quotes?+

Yes — every quote is free and there's no obligation to book. Use the form on this page or call us; we'll typically reply within one business day.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. We're a fully licensed contractor and carry liability insurance up to $2M, plus workers' compensation. Documentation is available on request.

How long does a typical project take?+

It depends on scope. A single-room repaint is usually 1–2 days, a deck rebuild 3–5 days, and flooring varies by area. The quote includes a written timeline.

Do I need to be home during the work?+

Not necessarily. Many of our customers give us a key or smart-lock code. We send a daily progress update with photos either way.

What's your payment schedule?+

Typically: small deposit on signing, milestone payment at midpoint, final payment on completion and walk-through. Bank transfer and credit cards accepted.

What if I'm not happy with the work?+

We won't consider the job done until you've walked through and signed off. If there's an issue after, we come back and fix it — covered by our workmanship warranty.

Do you handle permits?+

When permits are required, we handle the application and inspection scheduling and roll the cost into the quote.
